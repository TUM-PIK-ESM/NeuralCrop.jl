# Climate processes

Climate routines read daily forcing and update rolling climate buffers used by phenology, temperature stress, snow, and soil-temperature calculations.

## Daily forcing

The daily weather state includes temperature, precipitation, shortwave radiation, longwave radiation, normalized forcing variables, daily CO2, and annual CO2.

## Climate buffers

Climate buffers store recent temperatures, monthly temperatures, annual temperatures, minimum-temperature summaries, and vernalization-related variables.

## Snow

Snow routines update snowpack, snow height, and snow fraction before the crop and soil processes are evaluated.
