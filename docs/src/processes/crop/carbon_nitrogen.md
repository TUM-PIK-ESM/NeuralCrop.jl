# Crop carbon and nitrogen

Crop carbon and nitrogen routines translate photosynthesis into biomass growth, pool allocation, respiration, nitrogen demand, and nitrogen uptake.

## Carbon

Carbon routines compute respiration, carbon allocation among crop pools, storage carbon, vegetation carbon, and biomass.

## Nitrogen

Nitrogen routines calculate total demand, leaf demand, uptake from soil pools, and nitrogen stress terms that feed back on crop growth.
