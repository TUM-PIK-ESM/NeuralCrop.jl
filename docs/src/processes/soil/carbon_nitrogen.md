# Soil carbon and nitrogen

Soil carbon and nitrogen routines update litter, fast, and slow pools while accounting for crop residue inputs, decomposition, tillage transfers, and environmental modifiers.

## Carbon pools

The model tracks litter carbon, fast carbon, slow carbon, decomposition fluxes, and heterotrophic respiration.

## Nitrogen pools

The model tracks litter nitrogen, fast nitrogen, slow nitrogen, ammonium, nitrate, nitrogen inputs, and transformations.

## Hybrid biogeochemistry

Hybrid functions can emulate decomposition or turnover components while retaining the larger pool-update structure.
