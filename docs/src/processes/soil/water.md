# Soil water

Soil water routines compute how precipitation, irrigation, infiltration, percolation, evaporation, and transpiration affect water content in each soil layer.

## Pedotransfer

`pedotransfer!` estimates hydraulic properties from soil texture and soil depth variables.

## Fluxes

Water fluxes include infiltration, percolation, layer outflow, surface runoff, and transpiration demand from crop roots.

## Hybrid water

Hybrid simulations can replace part of the soil water update with a neural moisture emulator.
