# Soil model

The soil model contains water, carbon, nitrogen, temperature, and snow-related state variables. Soil arrays are generally organized by soil layer and grid cell.

## Water

Soil water routines compute infiltration, percolation, evapotranspiration-related fluxes, runoff, free water, and layer water content.

## Biogeochemistry

Soil carbon and nitrogen routines update litter, fast, and slow pools. Tillage and crop residue inputs affect carbon and nitrogen transfers between pools.

## Temperature and snow

The model includes snow updates and a simplified lagged soil-temperature method. Soil temperature influences crop and soil process rates.
